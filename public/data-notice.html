How the rain chatter count works

What

The app counts public Bluesky posts that match a few rain and flood words together with "Barcelona". It counts them per hour, for the whole city.

What is kept

Hourly counts per word group, and nothing else. Counts under 5 are not shown.

Not kept: post text, links, authors, handles, images or location.

Posts that mention injury or health are dropped before counting.

Why

To notice heavy rain sooner, and to check official and press flood reports more often. It is not used to locate floods or to judge anyone.

Legal basis

We claim legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). A balancing test is being drafted. This is a hackathon prototype and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

Opting out

Your account can be excluded from the count. To do that, the team stores only a one-way hash of your account id.

Contact details will be added before this runs outside the hackathon.

Source

Bluesky public search API.
